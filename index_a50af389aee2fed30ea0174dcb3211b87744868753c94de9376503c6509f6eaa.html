01 About

Engineer between electrons and physiology.

I'm Stefan, a biomedical engineer (Dipl.-Ing.) and PhD researcher in integrated sensors at the Institute of Measurement Technology in Leoben. Before that I worked as a Test Engineer at Meon Medical Solutions and spent three years as a test engineering intern at Infineon while finishing my studies.

Most of my projects sit where hardware meets the human body: sensors that read eye movement, stimulation that moves a hand, classifiers that decode EEG — and the test stations that make sure all of it works, every time.
